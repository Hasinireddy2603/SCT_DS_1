# SCT_DS_1

## SkillCraft Technology - Data Science Internship

### Task 01 - Population Data Visualization

This project was completed as part of the SkillCraft Technology Data Science Internship.

The objective of this task was to create a bar chart to visualize the distribution of a continuous variable using population data.

## Dataset

The dataset was obtained from the World Bank.

**Indicator:** Population, total (SP.POP.TOTL)

**Data Source:** World Bank

## Objective

The objective of this task is to visualize and compare the population of the top 10 most populous countries using a bar chart.

## Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

## Methodology

1. Retrieved population data from the World Bank.
2. Explored the dataset using Pandas.
3. Checked and handled missing population values.
4. Extracted country names from the World Bank data.
5. Removed regional and income-group aggregates.
6. Selected the latest available year.
7. Sorted countries based on population.
8. Selected the top 10 most populous countries.
9. Converted population values into millions.
10. Created a bar chart using Matplotlib.
11. Added population values above the bars for better readability.

## Visualization

The bar chart shows the population of the top 10 most populous countries in the latest available year (2025).

The visualization makes it easier to compare population sizes between countries.

## Key Observations

- India has the highest population among the selected top 10 countries.
- China has the second-highest population.
- India and China have considerably larger populations than the other countries in the top 10.
- The United States ranks third among the selected countries.
- Indonesia, Pakistan, Nigeria, and Brazil have populations above 200 million.
- Bangladesh, the Russian Federation, and Ethiopia have populations below 200 million among the selected top 10 countries.
- The chart shows significant variation in population size across countries.
- A bar chart provides a simple and effective way to compare population sizes.

## Conclusion

The visualization demonstrates the large differences in population among the world's most populous countries. The bar chart provides a clear and effective way to compare population sizes and understand the distribution of population across countries.
